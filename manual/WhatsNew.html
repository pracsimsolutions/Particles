What's New in Particles

Release notes for each version of the Particles module. Newest at the top.

Particles 26.0.1 (June 26, 2026)

  • Disabled toggle. Turn an emitter off (no particles) without deleting it; its handle stays visible so you can re-enable it.
  • Per-emitter handle visibility. Each emitter's handle now honors its own hide-shape, under the system's global Show Planes / Show Arrows.
  • Fix. The particle system no longer leaves other objects in the model looking unlit or untextured.
